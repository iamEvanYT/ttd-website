Season Pass Update is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

The new season pass is will rotate every month with a new theme!

This theme is clocks!

You can earn season pass XP in every mode.

You can also get XP boosts from completing quests!

---

# Season Pass Units

## Fallen Clockman (Godly) (Level 50)

This is the final unit you get for completing the season pass!

This unit slashes units with piercing.

The Fallen Shadow ability has a 300 second cooldown and lasts 30 seconds.

It will move toilets backwards for the 30 seconds under a certain strength of around 1 million.

All other toilets will be slowed by 75%. On top of this all toilets will take 3x the amount of damage.

## Rainbox Future Large Clockman (Godly) (Level 40)

This is the brother of Golden Future Large Clockman that also has a time stop ability.

This ablitity does a little higher damage thats multiplied during the time stop.

This unit punches units and slows them. There is also a chance too turn back units.

## Clock Clockman (Mythic) (Level 30)

This unit punches toilets and has a laser beam

The laser will push back toilets for 5 seconds that are under 500k strength.

The punches slow toilets aswell.

## Sword Clockman (Legendary) (Level 20)

Has a sword that he strikes toilets with.

Toilets will be slowed by 90% this will happen.

They can only be slowed up too 10 seconds in total.

## Bomb Cameraman

Punches toilets

Has a bomb head that explodes every minute that does splash damage

All toilets effected are slowed by 50% for 20 seconds.
