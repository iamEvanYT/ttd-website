# <u>Clock Mode V2</u>

Get ready for a time-twisting adventure with Clock Mode V2! It's a blast from the past, but with a super cool twist. The magnet toilets now have an awesome new power! They can teleport units to anywhere on the map!

What's in it for you? If you beat this mode, you'll get a **FREAKY Clock Crate**! Here's what you might find inside:

###### Odds:

- 75% - Toy Clockman
- 24% - Knee Surgery Clockman
- 1% - Chill Clock Guy

## Toy Clockman (75% - Legendary)

This little guy swings a bouncy hammer that sends toilets flying backwards! 💥  
He starts with 10k strength and can go all the way up to 100k!

## Knee Surgery Clockman (24% - Mythic)

Watch out! This Clockman stabs toilets with a syringe! 🤕  
He has a 10% chance to grab a unit and stab it 10 times for 10x damage!  
If a toilet survives, it gets slowed by 75% forever! 🐢  
Starts at 10k strength and can reach up to 250k!

## Chill Clock Guy (1% - Godly)

This laid-back dude strolls in and has a 1% chance to turn toilets into allies! 😲  
Plus, he can stop time for 20 seconds and deal 25,000 damage per wave! Talk about a power move! 💥🕒

---

# <u>Other Cool Units</u>

## Corrupted Titan Clock Man (Exclusive)

Punches units with mega-strength!  
Shoots orbs that do splash damage and might even send units back!  
Plus, he can freeze time for 15 seconds! ❄️

## Mystical Titan (Godly)

A magical tree that spawns butterflies to attack toilets in its range!  
Some butterflies can slow down toilets and do splash damage when they’re defeated! 💥
