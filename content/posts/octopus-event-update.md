The Octopus Event is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

## Green Light Map

This new map has a new exciting feature! You better make sure not too move during the red light!  
If you do something very bad will happen too you and your units!  
This will definitly be a challenge for some.

If you complete this map 3 times you will recieve 1 octopus crate free!

# Octopus Crate (500 Gems)

###### Odds:

- 75% - Shrimp Speakerman
- 20% - Crab Cameraman
- 4.9% - Octopus TV Man
- 0.1% - Backman

## Shrimp Speakerman (75%)

Shoots toilets with a pistol, then gets an automatic pistol as it levels.

## Crab Cameraman (20%)

Has a MP5 that it shoots toilets with! Later it gets a suppressor.

## Octopus TV Man (4.9%)

Shoots toilets with a sniper, this unit has massive range and is much stronger than the crab and octopus!

## Backman (0.1%)

Has a full auto pistol. Gets a second one as it levels up.

Level 1: Spawns Shrip speakermen

Level 2: Spawns Crab camerman

Level 3+: Spawns Octopus TV Men

# Tech Event Pass Units

## Robot Cameraman

Shoots fire out of its chest this does AOE damage!

Has a laser it shoots out of its palm

Later gets a second laser that it fires from both hands with.

## Electric TV Man

Mews at toilets that stuns them up too 5 seconds.

Has a laser that does static damage meaning it will also effect other units in range.

Every 30 seconds this units will do a far AOE attack that slows toilets for 60% up too 5 seconds.
