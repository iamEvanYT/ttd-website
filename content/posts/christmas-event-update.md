The Christmas Event is Here in Toilet Tower Defense!

Get ready for a festive, action-packed update with exciting new features, challenging gameplay, and tons of rewards! Check out all the new additions below:

---

# <u>Christmas Event</u>

## Santa's Workshop Map

This map has two difficulties, Easy and Hard!

**Easy Mode**: A fun and accessible challenge for everyone.  
**Hard Mode**: A tough challenge with a twist! Every 5 waves, your units have a 50% chance to freeze, but they’ll thaw out after 5 waves. However, they may freeze again, so stay on your toes!

Bonus: Santa has some tasks for you in this mode!  
New Enemy: Watch out for the Krampus Toilet, a speedy new foe!

![](/blog/images/2024/12/Screenshot-2024-12-14-at-4.50.29-PM.webp)

## Christmas Tree

Help Elf Speakerwoman prepare the Christmas Tree by completing tough challenges, collecting ornaments, and finding missing gifts scattered across various areas, including:

- The lobby
- The trading plaza
- The Drill World lobby
- Santa's Workshop Map!

**Rewards:** Completing the tree tasks will grant you two exciting new rewards!

# <u>Christmas Shop</u>

Get into the holiday spirit with the new shop offerings!

![](/blog/images/2024/12/image-2.webp)

## Christmas Crate

Including the new Christmas Crate, which contains a bunch of festive units!

###### Odds:

- 50% - Candy Cane Cameraman (Epic)
- 45% - Present Speakerman (Legendary)
- 5% - Titan Snowman (Mythic)
- 1% - Skibidi Claus (Godly)
- 0.05% - Titan Glacier TV Man (Ultimate)

## Candy Cane Cameraman (Epic - 50%)

A festive version of the classic cameraman, armed with a candy cane sword.

## Present Speakerman (Legendary - 45%)

Spawns mini Speakermen that follow the track and deal double damage based on their health!

## Titan Snowman (Mythic - 5%)

Throws snowballs at toilets with a 1% chance to freeze them in an ice cube for 10 seconds.  
Strengths increase with each level: 100k, 250k, 500k, 750k, 1m, and 1.5m.

## Skibidi Claus (Godly - 1%)

Throws special gifts on the track:  
Normal gift (75%) – standard gift.  
Blue gift (15%) – explodes on destruction, causing splash damage and slowing toilets, with 2x the health of a normal gift.  
Red gift (9%) – a fiery gift with 5x the health of a normal gift.  
Black gift (1%) – an extremely tough gift that has 20x the health of a normal gift and sends units back to the start if they don’t destroy it.

## Titan Glacier TV Man (Ultimate - 0.05%)

Launches large ice attacks in an AOE style, slowing toilets by 60%.  
At level 3, gains a sword that stuns toilets for 6 seconds.  
At level 5, spawns powerful ice barriers.  
At level 9, unlocks the Freeze ability that freezes all toilets for 50 seconds (with a 500-second cooldown, acting as a time stop).

# <u>Christmas Tree Rewards</u>

## Frozen Speakerman (Mythic)

Throws ice cubes that slow toilets by 50% and deal splash damage.  
At level 3, gains a launcher with greater range!  
Unlocks a second launcher that halves cooldowns at higher levels.

## Gingerbread House (Godly)

A farm unit that generates 7000 money per wave with 4 max placements.  
Includes archers that shoot arrows at toilets.  
A nod to last year's Santa TV Man, this unit has 8 levels!
