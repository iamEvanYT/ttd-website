---

# <u>Halloween Event</u>

The lobby has become halloween-themed!  
There is a special halloween mode, which you can earn candy on!  
Play it and complete halloween quests to increase your halloween level.  
Level 5 and 10 grant limited-time halloween units!

![](/blog/images/2024/10/RobloxScreenShot20241012_115026193.webp)

## Candy Launcher Cameraman

## (Halloween Level 5)

This speakerman launches candy at toilets.  
The candy is actually EXPLODING candy! Very bad to eat, but very good for fighting toilets!

## Vampire Cameraman

## (Halloween Level 10)

Watch out toilets! This vampire is hungry for skibidi blood.  
Every time this cameraman dashes forward to attack, the toilet has a 5% chance to turn into an ally and fight for you! (Only toilets under 1M Strength / Strength = Base HP x Speed)

## Halloween Crate

There is also a Halloween 2024 crate! This crate has 4 units.

🏠 **Trick or Treat Cameraman** (Uncommon / 75%)  
A timed farm - this cameraman goes between houses to collect candy!  
He is also a businessman, so he instantly sells the candy for money!

🐴 **Headless Speakerman** (Epic / 20%)  
This speakerman lost his head, so he rides around on a speaker horse hunting toilets!  
He dashes forward to attack with a pretty decent range (since he has a horse).  
He also gets an exploding pumpkin which deals splash damage at later levels!

📺 **Evil TV Man** (Legendary / 5%)  
Watch out! This TV Man is evil! Luckily, he only tries to fight toilets.  
He does a bit of AoE damage!

🧙 **Witch Camerawoman** (Godly / 0.5%)  
This is a very versatile support unit! She brews multiple potions which do cool things!  
She has these chances to throw each potion:  
(50%) Acid Potion - Does splash damage and 5 ticks of 10% of the splash damage!  
(48%) Slowing Potion - Slows all toilets in splash radius by up to 50% (increases each level)!  
(1%) Damage Potion - All toilets hit by this absorb up to 50% (increases each level) extra damage!  
(1%) Death Potion - Does 10x the normal splash damage!

# <u>Goal Crate</u>

There is also a new feature - the goals board! Located above the spawn on the arch that's on the way to endless mode, this board has 3 goals:

- Like goal (every 500 likes)
- Favorites goal (every 1,000 favorites)
- Group members (every 2,000 group members)

When a goal is hit, everyone online (in lobby, plaza, game, or afk zone) gets a goal crate!  
You can toggle the goal crate text in settings.

###### Odds:

- 99% - Thumbs Up Speakerman
- 1% - Titan Heart Man

![](/blog/images/2024/10/RobloxScreenShot20241012_120954109.webp)

## **Thumbs Up Speakerman (99% / Rare)**

Hits toilets with a green thumb he holds!

## **Titan Heart Man (1% / Mythic)**

Has a bunch of speakers - but unlocks a sword in middle levels!  
The only thing he doesnt love is TOILETS!!!!! 😠

# <u>Exclusive Units</u>

## Zombie Horde Cameraman (Exclusive)

This unit can be found in the Exclusive Shop for 399 Robux.

Spawns zombies to fight for you! The weaker zombies punch, bomb zombies explode upon death, and the boss zombies have powerful swords!  
Level 1: 5 Zombies  
Level 2: 3 Strong Zombies  
Level 3: 3 Strong Zombies  
Level 4: Boss Zombie + 4 Bomb Zombies  
Level 5: Upgraded Boss Zombie + 2 Boss Zombies + 3 Bomb Zombies

## Chair (Exclusive)

This unit can be found in the Exclusive Shop for 399 Robux.

Flies around hitting toilets!  
Flies so fast toilets get confused by what hit them - 50% slowing for 10 seconds when hit!
