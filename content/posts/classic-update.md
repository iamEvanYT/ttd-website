Hello everyone, the Classic Update is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

## Classic Map

Play the original toilet city map in abysmal mode, by heading to the event area.

- Carnival tickets can be earnt through this map as well, to unlock carnival units!

---

## Classic Crate

This crate costs 200 gems and the following units can be found inside:

- Bubble Blaster TV Man
  - This is a mythical unit that deals up to 47,000 damage by shooting bubble blasts at its enemies.
- Plumber Cameraman
  - This is another mythical unit that deals up to 45,000 damage by whacking its enemies with a pipe.
- Low Flow TV Man
  - This is a Godly unit that deals up to 165,000 damage while hurling toilet seats towards its enemies

---

## Exclusive Units

### Titan Bucket Head Speaker Woman

This Ultimate unit can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for R$399. It contains serials and is a very powerful ultimate that shoots a toilet beam from its head.

### Titan Plunger Cameraman

This Ultimate unit can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for R$349. It contains serials and is a unit of mass melee destruction.
