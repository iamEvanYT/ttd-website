Hello toilet warriors, the second season of the season pass is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# Season Pass Season Two

This season will be much more of a grind but the rewards will be worth it!

Some of the later units will be meta especially if you are a newer player!

---

# Robber Cameraman (Epic) (Level 10 Reward):

Stabs toilets with a big knife to stop them! 🔪🚽

# Burglar TV Man (Legendary) (Level 20 Reward):

- Does 360 degree AOE!
- Spins around and shoots toilets with his pistol! 🔫💥

# Mob Boss Toilet (Mythic) (Level 30 Reward):

- Fires huge rockets at toilets that explode and cause big damage! 🚀💣
- Has splash damage 💦

# Cop Cameraman (Godly) (5 Levels):

- Uses his baton to hit toilets, doing lots of damage (up to 150k DPS)! 🦹‍♂️💥

He can hit a lot of toilets at once and even go through them! ⚡

# Titan Swat Cameraman (6 Levels) (Godly):

- Calls for backup and brings more Cop Cameramen to help! 👮‍♂️👮‍♂️
- Boosts the range of everyone’s attacks by 20%! 🚀
