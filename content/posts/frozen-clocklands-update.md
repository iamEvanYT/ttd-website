Hello everyone, the Frozen Clocklands is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# <u>Frozen Clocklands Map</u>

This is a new map with a HUGE reward of a Frozen Clocklands Crate and a chance for Evolve Shards and Toilet Shards.

You can find it next to Palm Paradise!

---

# <u>Frozen Clocklands Crate</u>

This crate is awarded whenever you beat the new Frozen Clocklands Map. Additionally, you can also purchase the crate in the Exclusive Shop for 150 Gems.

###### Odds:

- Timeworn Clock Watcher (60%)
- Frosted Clock Gearling (35%)
- Titan Shiverguard Clockman (4.99%)
- Titan Cryo Clock Striker (0.01%)

## Timeworn Clock Watcher (60%)

A lumbering, ancient mech! Smashes the ground with both fists in a small AOE once he reaches the front lines.

## Frosted Clock Gearling (35%)

A frosted sentry made of gears! Freezes enemies in their tracks with an ice beam.

## Titan Shiverguard Clockman (4.99%)

A towering clock knight! Slashes through enemies with spinning blades and drops shield barriers to block toilets.

## Titan Cryo Clock Striker (0.01%)

An **ULTIMATE** cold-blooded spear titan! Dashes forward and pierces every toilet in his path with icy precision.

# <u>Exclusive Items</u>

## Chrono Clockman (Unit)

This unit can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for R$9999.  
It is the first unit to only be avalible for 24 hours!  
It is also the first exclusive unit to have serials!

A time-twisted warlock! Conjures icy beams and frosted scythes from his body, hurling them at high speeds to slice down his foes.
