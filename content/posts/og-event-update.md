Hello Toilet Slayers, The OG Event 2.0 is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# The OG Lobby!

From the OG lobby you can go play 4 of the original maps!

These will play on different difficulties and winning them will earn you OG Coins!

---

# Controllable Units

We have a new feature that lets you control your own units!

This currently is only available with the new POV Cameraman Godly in the OG Camera Crate.

When you place this new unit you will be able to go over and press E to control it.

From here you will be able to fire and turn it however you would like!

There will be more units and gamemodes that use this new feature in the future.

(For PC Users: Use right click to aim in more, hold left click on higher upgraded weapons to automatically fire!)

(For Mobile Users: Hover crosshair over toilets to automatically shoot!)

# OG Camera Crate (200 OG Coins)

## Dark Blue Suit Cameraman (35%) (Rare)

- Has the plasma disabler gun which stuns toilets for up to 4 seconds!

## Detective Cameraman (30%) (Epic)

- Has the plasma disabler gun which stuns toilets for up to 6 seconds!
- Shoots with shorts bursts that will hit many toilets around its inital target.
- Has a 1 percent chance for units under 100k strength to take 10% of their max health!

## Lucky Cameraman (15%) (Legendary)

- Has the plasma disabler gun which stuns toilets for up to 7 seconds!
- Shoots with shorts bursts that will hit many toilets around its inital target.
- Has a 1 percent chance for units under 200k strength to take 10% of their max health!
- Does AOE aswell that has a 1 percent chance to turn any toilet to an ally!

## Speaker Van (15%) (Legendary)

- Spawns allies out of the back door!
- Spawns speakerman and large speakermen!

## Camera Dropship (4%) (Legendary)

- Drops allies down!
- Spawns speakerman, large speakermen, titan cameramen, and upgraded titan cameramen!
- Will heal the allies by 25% at the start of every wave!

## POV Cameraman (1%) (Godly)

- Starts with a pistol, then gets a SMG, then and assualt rifle then on the final level explosive bullets (Splash)!
- The SMG and assualt rifle are automatic so you only need to hold down left click if you are on PC!
- Has the new control feature!
