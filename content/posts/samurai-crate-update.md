The Samurai Crate is here in Toilet Tower Defense! Along with a few improvements and stuff for endless!

---

# New Endless Ranks

- Rank 16, Toilet Wizard (500k XP): Unlock x3 Speed
- Rank 17, Toilet Superstar (600k XP): +1 Extra Placement Slot and +$100 Starting Money in Endless
- Rank 18, Toilet Guru (750k XP): Extra Slot on Hotbar in Endless Mode & 10% more coins on Endless Mode
- Rank 19, Toilet Champion (900k XP): +$200 Starting Money in Endless & +1 Extra Placement Slot
- Rank 20, Ultimate Toilet God (1.1M XP): "Friend Maker" ability - One Time Use

The Friend Maker Ability takes the furthest toilet and turns it into an ally! So it will help you!

On top of the new ranks the scaling has change and some of the later waves have been changed as well.

---

# New Endless Leaderboard Reward

## 🕰️ Sinister Titan Clockman

You will recieve this ultimate if you place in the top 100 of endless players!

This unit does 250k DPS!

He also has a minute long black hole time stop! With a cooldown of 500 seconds. All toilets will not move for an entire minute!

He slashes through toilets doing slowing and picks them up stunning them up to 10 seconds.

The Sinister Titan Clockman also places black holes around the map, these will send toilets back to the start.

---

# Unit Manager

There is a new unit manager in matches! This will allow you to see all of you units you have placed.

From there you can upgrade, sell them, or select them.

You can also see how much the unit will sell for.

This is helpful if you have many units that are close together or are moving!

# Samurai Crate (500 Gems)

## 🗣️ Samurai Speakerman (65%)

Slams toilets with a sledge hammer hitting multiple toilets that surround it main target!

## 📷 Shogun Cameraman (34%)

Red and Gold Shogun much more powerful than the training Samurai Speakerman!

A dark aura surronds him pushing back toilets in a 360 degree radius around him!

Slices through toilets with 2 swords!

## 🥷 Titan Samurai Speakerwoman (1%)

Slashes through toilets piercing though many at a single time!

Spawns orbs that are explosive and do splash damage!

10% chance to INSTANTLY kill a toilet under 10% health (every second). This attack can do over a million damage under the right circumstances.

# Other Cool Units

## Titan Samurai Cameraman

Has a single katana that he slashes toilets with doing slowing up to 50%!

Has 3 different types of orbs that he throws

- Red orb: Does splash damage and 5 ticks of 10% of the splash damage!
- Black orb: Does 10X the amound of damage to toilets in the splash radius.
- Green orb: All toilets hit by this absorb up to 50% (increases each level) extra damage!
