# <u>Thanksgiving Event</u>

## Thanksgiving Mode

Play a harder version of the thanksgiving mode to earn TURKEYS!  
Use turkeys in the event shop (triangle area near spawn) to buy turkey crates.  
You can also get 2x turkey boosts for a little gems!

## Ultra Turkey Crate

Contains Turkey Units for this event!

###### Odds:

- 50% - Turkey TV Man (Rare)
- 40% - Turkey Drillman (Epic)
- 9% - Turkey Clockman (Legendary)
- 1% - Turkey Herder Speakerman (Mythic)
- 0.1% - Titan Turkey Man (Godly)

## Turkey TV Man (Rare)

Weaker TV Man, does AoE damage.

## Turkey Drillman (Epic)

Hits toilets with turkey drills!

## Turkey Clockman (Legendary)

TURKEY BOMB!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

## Turkey Herder Speakerman (Mythic)

he likes turkeys. he has hundreds of them as pets!  
he also sends them to war - to fight the toilets.

## Titan Turkey Man (Godly)

BIG TURKEY MAN!! turkey sword, turkey blasters, and even placing turkeys makes this the most turkeyest unit.

# <u>Microphone Crate</u>

**New gem crate!**  
You can purchase it for 300 Gems each in the Exclusive Shop.  
These units mostly generate sound waves to do static or AoE damage.

###### Odds:

- 40% - Microphone Man
- 30% - Shield Microphone Man
- 25% - Bass Microphone Man
- 5% - Amplifier Microphone Man
- 0.5% - Titan Microphone Man

## Microphone Man

Basic static damage.

## Shield Microphone Man

Basic static damage, but can reflect toilet attacks!

## Bass Microphone Man

AoE slowing 360 degrees!

## Amplifier Microphone Man

Boosts ALL stats!

## Titan Microphone Man

Really big microphone man with quick swords, range boosting, and an ability to disrupt nearby toilets!
