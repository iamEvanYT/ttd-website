Hello everyone, the Wastelands Update is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# <u>Wastelands Map</u>

This is a new map slightly harder than Hard Mode! You can find it in the Lobby next to Frozen Clocklands.

---

# <u>Wastelands Crate</u>

This crate is awarded whenever you beat the new Wastelands Map. Additionally, you can also purchase the crate in the Exclusive Shop for 300 Gems.

###### Odds:

- Irradiated Cameraman (70%)
- Hazmat Operator (25%)
- Wasteland Enforcer (3.99%)
- Gamma Ray Sniper (0.01%)

## Irradiated Cameraman (70%)

A twisted version of a once-heroic unit! Twitchy and unstable, it lunges forward to blast enemies with a corrosive green beam from its head.

## Hazmat Operator (25%)

A double-handed danger! Stands firm and launches radioactive blasts from both palms to melt through toilet swarms.

## Wasteland Enforcer (3.99%)

An unforgiving cyborg of corrupted order! Smashes the ground and sweeps enemies aside with sheer force.

## Gamma Ray Sniper (0.01%)

A mechanical sharpshooter! Fires powerful sniper rounds with a heavy kick and lunges in close with a hidden energy blade when enemies get too close.

This unit can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for R$9999.  
It is only avalible for a week with a serial!

The apex of radioactive power! Rains down missiles and unleashes a devastating energy beam from his head to wipe out entire lanes.

## Chrono Skull (Exclusive Unit)

This is available in the Exclusive Shop for R$599!

# <u>Others</u>

## Radioactive (Status Effect)

Slows enemies by 75% for 5 seconds. During this time, 10 ticks of damage equivalent to the sum of 1/2 of the unit's attack is dealt to each/any affected toilet.

Affected units will be surrounded by a toxic flame!

## Bug Fixes and Improvements

- Chrono Clockman has been buffed + added **infinite** blocks against stuns!
- Chrono Skull has also been buffed on most stats!
- Fixed frozen clocklands rewards
