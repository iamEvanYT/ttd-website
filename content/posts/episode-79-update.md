# <u>Ruined Crate</u>

This crate can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for 250 Gems.

###### Odds:

- Upgraded Toilet Scientist (73.5%)
- Mafia Mutant (25%)
- Titan CH4D Cameraman (1.49%)
- TitanAstroDuchess (0.01%)

## Upgraded Toilet Scientist (73.5% - Legendary)

This unit has a powerful laser used to damage toilets!

## Mafia Mutant (25% - Mythic)

Fitted with a reinforced laser array, it slices through normal toilets in seconds.

## Titan CH4D Cameraman (1.49% - Godly)

Its overcharged laser lance liquefies bunkers and armored columns alike, leaving molten trenches where defensive lines used to be.

## Titan Astro Duchess (0.01% - Ultimate)

This super-laser can demolish toilets in seconds!
