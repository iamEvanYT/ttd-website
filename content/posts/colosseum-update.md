Hello everyone, the Colosseum Update is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# <u>Colosseum Crate</u>

This crate can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for 400 Gems.

###### Odds:

- Sandshield Soldier (70%)
- Bladestorm Brute (26%)
- Oracle Speakerman (3.995%)
- Titan Colosseus Maximus (0.005%)

## Sandshield Soldier (70% - Legendary)

A sandy Roman defender! Strikes with a bronze tower shield, knocking back toilets around him.

## Bladestorm Brute (26% - Mythic)

An unstoppable whirlwind of blades! Spins with twin gladii to shred groups of toilets in a brutal 360° slash

## Oracle Speakerman (3.995% - Godly)

A divine warrior of fate! Launches electrified spears that pierce through multiple toilets in a line.

## Titan Colosseus Maximus (0.005% - Celestial)

Champion of the colosseum! Wields giant axes in crushing sweeps and hurls a chained flail at distant enemies. Roars to stun everything nearby—nearly unstoppable.

# <u>Exclusive Items</u>

## Brutus The Bound (Exclusive Unit)

This unit can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for R$699.

A chained colossus of pain! Pounds the ground with massive fists in wide AoE slams and lunges forward with explosive momentum every third strike.

# <u>Others</u>

## Changes

- Players from regions with paid random items being restricted will have access to exclusive units, but won't receive shinies (this is explicitly mentioned in the shop).
- Chrono clockman deals Splash damage instead of AOE (this inconsistency is fixed)
- Cost of upgrading Omega Reactor Overlord adjusted
- Omega Reactor Overlord removed from the shop/game
- Speedrun mode loadout has been refreshed!

## Bug Fixes and Improvements

- Attack radius circle moves with the unit for Chrono Skull and Wasteland Enforcer
- Recent crates will appear in the correct order in inventory
- Some recent units were improperly ordered in inventory, this is fixed
- Traits will show in trade window
- Traits will show in "sell units" section in the trading plaza
- Fixed inability to enter pro plaza while owning some new ultimate/celestial units
- Wastelands map now awards XP + season pass boost XP for winning the map
