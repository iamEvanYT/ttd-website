# <u>4th of July Event</u>

## 4th of July Crate

A new event crate is here! You can purchase this in the **Exclusive Shop**, or get one for free **every hour**!

###### Odds:

- 99.95% - Sparkler Cameraman
- 0.05% - Titan Firework Man

![](/blog/images/2024/07/image-1.webp)

## Free July 4th Crates

Every hour (until the event ends), you would get a free July 4th Crate if you're in game! Additionally, there is a countdown & a firework show in the lobby!

![](/blog/images/2024/07/RobloxScreenShot20240703_231137855.webp)

## Sparkler Cameraman

This **Uncommon** unit has 3 sparklers, White, Blue and Red and they deal different types of damage!  
The White Sparkler does piercing damage, Blue Sparkler (on the back) does 360° AOE and the Red Sparkler does Splash Damage!

![](/blog/images/2024/07/RobloxScreenShot20240703_175230332.webp)

## Titan Firework Man

This titan has 5 levels and it shoots a lot of fireworks! All the fireworks do splash damage, so it is very powerful! It also have a really cool spawn animation & it can be obtained in the **4th of July Crate**.

![](/blog/images/2024/07/RobloxScreenShot20240703_223832820.webp)

# <u>Episode 75</u>

## Exclusive Fan Crate

A new crate is now up for sale in the Exclusive Shop! It containers 3 different fan units which can push toilet back with their fans!

###### Odds:

- 60% - Fanman
- 39% - Large Fanman
- 1% - Titan Fanman

![](/blog/images/2024/06/image-4.webp)

## Beret Cameraman

This is a new **Exclusive** Unit! It has a lot of explosives in its pocket, so be careful! It will unlock new explosives every upgrade. It can be found in the **Exclusive Shop** for **399 Robux**.

![](/blog/images/2024/07/RobloxScreenShot20240703_224244343.webp)

## Military Mutant Toilet Girl

The **Mythic** Military Mutant Toilet Girl, who appeared on Episode 75 is now available in Summon! It has arm lasers, claws and armor availble when you upgrade it!

![](/blog/images/2024/07/RobloxScreenShot20240702_234215145.webp)

## Arachnid Militant Toilet

This is also a **Mythic** Unit! It has 4 claws, laser cannons and buzzsaws! It also has buzzsaws to heavily damage toilets! This unit can be found in Summon.

![](/blog/images/2024/07/RobloxScreenShot20240702_234458192.webp)
