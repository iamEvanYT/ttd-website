---

# <u>Fireworks</u>

There are fireworks in the lobby which goes off every hour! It will reward you with a 2025 Independence Day Crate.

---

# <u>Titan Freedom Speakerman</u>

There is a 1% chance of getting this unit in the crate. This unit has strong speakers that damages toilets!

---

# <u>American Speakerman</u>

There is a 99% chance of getting this unit in the crate. This unit uses knifes to damage toilets!
