Hello Toilet Slayers, The Mafia Crate is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# Mafia Boss Cameraman Questline

Help the Mafia Boss Cameraman to unlock a new limited time mount!

To help complete 3 short quests in order:

- Equip a Mafia unit.
- Defeat 100 toilets with Mafia units.
- Get 3 wins with only Mafia units equipped.

---

# Mafia Crates (200 Gems)

## Speaker Henchman (50%) (Epic)

- Punches Toilets with his fist!
- Gets a switchblade at level 2 and stabs toilets.

## Camera Henchman (30%) (Legendary)

- Has a Tommy gun that he shoots toilets with.
- The Tommy gun gets more powerful as he is leveled!

## TV Henchman (19%) (Mythic)

- Has great 360 degree AOE damage!
- Has a sniper that shoots once a second.
- At level 3 he gets a scope which increases his range.

## Mafia Boss Cameraman (1%) (Godly)

- Is a damage booster! Boosts up to 30% damage to units in his range.
- Has a golden tommy gun that he shoots toilets with.
- Spawns speaker henchmen to help fight!

---

# Other Cool Units

## Printer Henchman (Exclusive) (399 Robux)

- Is a money printer, prints at max $3000 every 30 seconds
- Limits of placing 2.
- Has a silenced SMG that he shoots toilets with.
