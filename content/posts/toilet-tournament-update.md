Hello everyone, the Toilet Tournament is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# <u>Toilet Tournament</u>

Get ready to participate in 7 different tournaments with different goals! You will be fighting against 199 random players. Each tournament lasts 24 hours, and you can earn points by following the goals! Get on the top of the leaderboard for rewards, or complete milestones to unlock reward chests!

![](/blog/images/2025/07/image.webp)

---

# <u>Titan Reaper Cameraman (Ultimate)</u>

This unit is a reward for being #1 - #3 on the Tournament Leaderboard. It has a scythe with freezing abilities.

---

# <u>Titan Reaper Speakerman (Godly)</u>

This unit is a reward for being #4 - #25 on the Tournament Leaderboard. It has a powerful scythe to attack toilets.

---

# <u>Exclusive Units</u>

## Titan Reaper TV Man (Exclusive)

This Exclusive unit can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for R$399.

It has a large scythe which has attacks with radiation and piercing.

This is only available for a week!
