Hello everyone, Part 2 of the Grow a Toilet Event is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# <u>New Toilets and Eggs</u>

There are 8 new toilets and 2 new eggs available now!

![](/blog/images/2025/07/image-6.webp)

---

# <u>New Exclusive Units (TTD)</u>

## Titan Abomination

This Celestial unit is available in the Exclusive Shop for 779 robux! It also includes serials!

## Disaster Scientist Mech

This Exclusive unit is available in the Exclusive Shop for 279 robux!

---

# <u>Gear Shop</u>

You can now purchase **Boost Totems** from the gear shop which will increase the growth speed of the toilets!

![](/blog/images/2025/07/image-7.webp)

---

# <u>Toilet Mutations</u>

Toilets can now receive mutations! Mutations will increase the prices of toilets, which can make you richer quickly!

![](/blog/images/2025/07/image-3.webp)

---

# <u>Timed Events</u>

About every 30 minutes, there will be events happening which will randomly mutate some of your toilets!

![](/blog/images/2025/07/image-4.webp)

---

# <u>Toilet Gifting</u>

You can now be generous and gift toilet to other players!

![](/blog/images/2025/07/image-2.webp)

---

# <u>Toilet Index</u>

You can now see all the toilets you have ever collected!

![](/blog/images/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-19-at-5.35.53-PM.webp)

---

# <u>Favourite Items</u>

You can now right click on toilets in your inventory to favourite them to prevent them from being sold!

![](/blog/images/2025/07/image-5.webp)
