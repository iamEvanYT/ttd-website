# <u>Ranked</u>

Fight other players in a ranked version of 2v2 PVP!  
Join as a solo to get matched up with a teammate - or party up with a friend to queue as a duo!

Earn ranked coins to buy ranked crates! Rank up to get more ranked coins. The higher your rank the more ranked coins you get for winning/losing!  
The top 300 ranked players will get an ultimate (on rank lb reset every month) and a special mount (temporarily while on leaderboard, or permanently when it resets)!

![](/blog/images/2024/11/RobloxScreenShot20241109_151632418.webp)

## Parties

Party up with your friends!  
Invite people to parties, and when you go to a teleport or a queue, they will be counted as there with you!  
This button is at the top by quests.

# <u>Ranked Shop</u>

## Gladiator Crate

This is one of the crates in the Ranked Shop!

You can purchase it for 25 Ranked Coins Each.

###### Odds:

- 80% - Gladiator Speakerman (Epic)
- 19% - Chariot Cameraman (Legendary)
- 1% - Barbarian Large TV Man (Mythic)
- 0.1% - Titan Centurion Speakerman (Godly)
- 0.002% - Titan Warlord Clockman (Ultimate)

## Golden Gladiator Crate

Both of these crates have the same units - but this crate has better chances!

You can purchase it for 100 Ranked Coins Each.

###### Odds:

- 15% - Gladiator Speakerman (Epic)
- 80% - Chariot Cameraman (Legendary)
- 4.5% - Barbarian Large TV Man (Mythic)
- 0.5% - Titan Centurion Speakerman (Godly)
- 0.01% - Titan Warlord Clockman (Ultimate)

## Gladiator Speakerman (Epic)

Speakerman with a sword and shield!

## Chariot Cameraman (Legendary)

A very fast movable unit - with splash damage!

## Barbarian Large TV Man (Mythic)

This TV man has two axes. He hits so hard, his axes can push toilets back!

## Titan Centurion Speakerman (Godly)

With a big lance, piercing damage, and DOUBLE damage to bosses, this titan is very strong.  
He can block a TON of stuns too, slow toilets, and even stun them!

## Titan Warlord Clockman (Ultimate)

4 hands. 360-degree AoE damage with slowing. Rewinding a toilet that reaches end back to the start. Clock turrets. Extra boss damage.

This unit has a ton of great stuff going for him - the toilets should be very scared.
