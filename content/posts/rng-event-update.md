# <u>RNG Event</u>

## RNG Mode

In this new game mode you earn dice that are used in the new RNG Shop

Instead of using your own units, **you roll** for the units you use!  
You can choose to use these units for the remainder of the match or discard them.

You can also use money too unlock more slots for the remainder of the match.  
The higher the rarity of unit the less odds there are for you to roll them.

## RNG Shop

The RNG Shop is here with a new crate, The RNG Crate!  
You can also get boosts here and dice to spend!

###### Odds:

- 50% - Magician Speakerman (Epic)
- 45% - Mystical Camerawoman (Legendary)
- 4.9% - Mage Cameraman (Mythic)
- 0.1% - Titan Dice Man (Godly)

## Magician Speakerman (Epic - 50%)

Launches various items at the toilets!  
There's a chance it could magically even launch a grenade! (Does blast damage)  
Or even a smaller chance to launch the man face!

## Mystical Camerawoman (Legendary - 45%)

Mystical looking camerawoman  
360 degree AoE circle

## Mage Cameraman (Mythic - 4.9%)

Spawns magic orbs that do various things and blast damage!  
At every level the orb changes.  
At higher levels these orbs slow toilets attacking.  
Also has a small chance to turn the toilets into an ally at these levels!

## Titan Dice Man (Godly - 0.1%)

Throws dice at toilets with a chance of them exploding!  
Has a sword that swings sideways that does AOE damage!  
Gets two cannons on the shoulder that fire also doing blast damage!

# <u>Episode 77</u>

## Samurai Cameraman (Exclusive)

This unit can be found in the Exclusive Shop for 599 Robux.

Slashs toilets with his samurai sword.  
After the initial stab, he does a sweep that does piercing damage!

# <u>Others</u>

## Dice Cameraman (Exclusive)

This RNG unit can be found in the Exclusive Shop for 599 Robux.  
Rolls dice that dictate how much damage toilets will take.  
The damage shown is the average of what rolls.  
Damages with lightning that also does splash damage.  
Starts with one dice, gets another at level 2, and at level 3 rolls 2 dice with each hand!
