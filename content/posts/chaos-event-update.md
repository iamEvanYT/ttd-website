Hello Toilet Slayers, The Chaos Event is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# Chaos Mode!

Chaos mode is a new mode with 2 different types of difficulties!

There is an easy and a hard mode.

For beating easy you will get 1 Chaotic Crate and for beating hard mode you will get 3 Chaotic Crates!

Every wave something will change making the game more difficult!

For example one wave your the toilets could come at twice the speed or you may loose a unit!

---

# Chaotic Crates (50 Gems)

## Unstable Speakerman (60%) (Rare)

- His weapon switches every 10 seconds
- Rotates between hitting with a sword or shooting with a rocket launcher!
- The rocket launcher will do splash damage!

## Chaotic Cameraman (30%) (Epic)

- Has an evit eye!
- This chaotic cameraman will slash at toilets!

## Glitched TV Man (1%) (Mythic)

- Has great AOE damage!
- Toilets have a chance to become corrupted then turn to allies!
- Only will affect weaker toilets below 500k strength.

## Titan Paradox Drillman (0.1%) (Godly)

- Slashes at toilets with his drill hands!
- These slashes can hit multiple toilets at once doing piercing!
- The drill on his back will fly off and explode doing splash damage!
- Can stun toilets for 10 seconds aswell!
