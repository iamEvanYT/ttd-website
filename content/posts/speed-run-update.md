Hello toilet warriors, the Speed Run Update is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# New Speed Run Mode!

In this mode you will use the same loadout as everyone, these units will rotate out weekly!

This way everyone has will be playing on an equal playing field.

There will be a top 100 leaderboard for this mode that also resets weekly!

The rewards are only gems currently but may be switched out in the future.

This mode will also take place on older maps that rotate out every few weeks.

In this mode you can skip faster and this will play on 2x speed!

If you win the match under a specified time you will get a free speed run crate!

---

# Speed Run Crate

## Titan Fast Armored Cameraman (Godly) (1%)

- Spawns speedy speakeren that run down the track attacking toilets
- Gains armor every upgrade making it stronger!
- Has an ability that speeds up all units by 2x speed within its radius. This will last for 30 seconds and have a 300 second cooldown

## Tracksuit Cameraman (Mythic) (25%)

- This speedy speakerman hits toilets while dashing at them super fast!
- All toilets that he hits will be slowed for 10 seconds by 60%.
- Has a 2% chance to send back toilets and transform them into allies!

## Speedy Speakerman (Legendary) (74%)

- This speedy speakerman hits toilets while dashing at them super fast!
- All toilets that he hits will be slowed for 3 seconds by 40%.

---

# Other Cool Units

## Zombie Toilet (Mythic)

- Bites toilets then and poisons them for 5 seconds! This will do an extra half the damage across the 5 seconds.
- Has 360 degree AOE where toilets in the area will be slowed for 8 seconds by 60%.

## Zombie Titan TV Man (Exclusive)

- Strikes toilets with a claw this swipes through multiple toilets at ones doing piercing!
- Has 360 degree AOE where toilets in the area will be slowed for 10 seconds by 60%.
