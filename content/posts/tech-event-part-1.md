# <u>Tech Event</u>

It sounds like there's a huge event happening in Toilet Tower Defense, full of exciting new content! Here's a breakdown of everything happening in the Tech Event:

## Tech City Map

- **Two difficulty levels**: Easy and Hard. Hard mode offers **2x batteries**.
- **New features**:
  - New toilets, the **hoverboard** and **computer toilet** that spawns hoverboards.
  - A **shop** with boosts and units to enhance your gameplay.

## Key Features of the Event

- **Extra Batteries** for completing scavenger hunts.
- **New Units and Traps** to help defend against the toilet invasion.
- New challenges in **Tech City** with increased rewards in **Hard mode**.

---

# <u>Tech Crate</u>

###### Odds:

- 50% - Computer Speakerman (Epic)
- 40% - Future Cameraman (Legendary)
- 9% - Titan Motherboard (Mythic)
- 1% - Cameramothership (Godly)

## Computer Speakerman (50% - Epic)

A normal AOE (area of effect) unit with a **2% chance to do 10x damage**.

## Future Cameraman (40% - Legendary)

Wields a **laser sword** and has a **10% chance to stun a toilet** for 10 seconds.

## Titan Motherboard (9% - Mythic)

A **farm unit** earning up to **$700 per round**.  
AOE attack with a **25% chance to set toilets on fire** for 5 seconds (slowing by 25%).  
Throws **glitch discs** with a 10% chance to drop a **green disc** doing **5x the damage**.

## Cameramothership (1% - Godly)

Spawns **untouchable units** that attack within the mothership’s range with various attacks.

---

# <u>Other Cool Units</u>

## Titan Forcefield Woman (Exclusive)

Similar to the Titan Forcefield Man, but connects with **other forcefields** in range.  
Creates an **AOE area** that **slows and damages** toilets.
