Hello everyone, the Summer Event Update is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# <u>Summer World</u>

Find a portal in the lobby to enter the **Summer World**! You can find two new maps in there which only allows summer units to be brought.

It also contains a new traits NPC that gives summer exclusive traits to summer units.

You also get a free summer unit when you first enter the world.

Have fun!

![](/blog/images/2025/06/image.webp)

---

# <u>Hot Summer Crate</u>

This crate can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for 8 Summer Tokens!

###### Odds:

- Titan Crocodile Speakerman (60%)
- Titan Shark Speakerman (20%)
- Titan Orca Speakerman (15%)
- Titan Kraken Speakerman (2.5%)
- Titan Leviathan Speakerman (2.5%)

## Titan Crocodile Speakerman (60%)

The swamp’s silent hunter! Launches explosive water jets from his jaws, tearing through toilets from afar.

## Titan Shark Speakerman (20%)

The ocean’s deadliest predator! Fires a razor-sharp water stream blades that slices clean through toilets.

## Titan Orca Speakerman (15%)

A titan of the seas! Shoots sonic water blasts that shatters oncoming toilets.

## Titan Kraken Speakerman (2.5%)

The mythic scourge of the deep! Unleashes inky energy projectiles that explode on contact, splashing damage across groups of toilets.

## Titan Leviathan Speakerman (2.5%)

The ancient ruler of the abyss! Fires massive tidal energy bursts that crush everything in its path with unstoppable force.

# <u>Summer Pass</u>

Open the Summer Pass with a button on the right side of your screen! It contains 40 levels currently and cool rewards for each of them such as tokens and crates.

---

# <u>Exclusive Units</u>

## Summer Juggernaut

This **CELESTIAL** unit can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for R$1899.

It has water blasts + smash attacks with cool effects. They also deal a high damage with splash attacks!

This is only available for a limited time!
