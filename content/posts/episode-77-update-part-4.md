---

# <u>Ultra Toilet Crate</u>

This is a new Summon Crate with 4 new toilets! It is better than the Advanced Toilet Crate & it cost 1000 coins each.

![](/blog/images/2024/11/image-1.webp)

## Cat Toilet (Epic - 60%)

This unit attacks very quickly! It dashes forward to attack when first spawned, and has 2 rocket launchers when upgraded.

![](/blog/images/2024/11/RobloxScreenShot20241102_140454583.webp)

## Upgraded Mafia Toilet (Legendary - 39%)

This unit has acid guns on both sides! It also has powerful lasers with a jetpack.

![](/blog/images/2024/11/RobloxScreenShot20241102_140700349.webp)

## Bald Toilet (Mythic - 1%)

This unit has two speakers with AOE ability! It also gains some glasses on max level to help it see clearly.  
Additionally, it can **block 100 stuns**!

![](/blog/images/2024/11/RobloxScreenShot20241102_140931392.webp)

## Upgraded Strider Toilet (Godly - 0.1%)

When first spawned, this unit has a small laser. When upgraded, it has two large lasers and one giant rocket with splash damage!

![](/blog/images/2024/11/RobloxScreenShot20241102_141243084.webp)

# <u>Others</u>

## Boss Toilet 5.0 (Exclusive)

This unit can be found in the Exclusive Shop for 599 Robux.

It has 3 powerful lasers and two claws for punching toilets.

It also has a special ability... It can spawn toilets as allies! They will spawn from the end of the track and walk to the toilet spawns.

![](/blog/images/2024/11/RobloxScreenShot20241102_160216588.webp)

## Juggernaut Titan TV Man (Exclusive)

This unit can be found in the Exclusive Shop for 699 Robux.

It has a slightly broken TV, a purple laser on its chest & a powerful claw!  
The claws can grab toilets under 2m toilet strength, and attack all toilets!

Strength = Base Toilet Health \* Walk Speed

![](/blog/images/2024/11/RobloxScreenShot20241102_160917908.webp)

## Controller Improvements

Controller support has improved!

- Press the Select key to enable the virtual cursor.
- Press 'Y' to Open Inventory
- Virtual Cursor automatically enables when a frame is open (Lobby Only)
- The Mounts & Partner Launcher buttons are now clickable on Controller!

![](/blog/images/2024/11/RobloxScreenShot20241102_161214945.webp)

## Bug Fixes & Improvements

- Only one side has to skip in PvP now!
- The Placement Preview Unit will now stick to your cursor.
