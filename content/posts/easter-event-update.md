Hello Toilet Slayers, The Easter Event is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# Egg Hunt

We have an egg hunt for you all!

There are 50 eggs hidden though-out the lobby, trading plaza, OG Lobby, and the maps.

These maps exclude: Pvp, Endless, Chaos mode or the Limited Time Modes!

If you collect 40 eggs you will recieve: Shell Speakerman (Mythic)

If you collect 50 eggs you will recieve: Titan Basketman (Godly)

---

# Easter Crate (100 Eggs)

## Bunny Rider Speakerman (50%) (Epic)

- Spawns bunnies as his allies!
- Dashes at toilets hitting them!

## Carrot Cameraman (40%) (Legendary)

- Throws carrots at toilets!

## Carrot Farmer Bunnyman (9%) (Mythic)

- A great farm.
- Cost a total of 4,800 to max out.
- Gives up to 2,500 per wave!

## Chickenstorm Drillman (1%) (Godly)

- Has really strong AOE! Over 100K DPS!
- Spawns in chickens that run around him assisting you in the fight!
- Spawns different eggs that act as barriers!
- White eggs are normal.
- Blue eggs explode and slow the toilets around it.
- Red eggs spawn with 5x the health!
- Black eggs act as a black hole and send toilets back to the spawn.

---

# Other Cool Units

## Titan Eggreaper (Exclusive) (599 Robux)

- Slashes toilets with a scythe!
- These slashes will hit multiple toilets around its target!
- The toilets hit by this will be slowed by 70%
- This unit also does 145k DPS!

## Shell Speakerman (Mythic) (40 / 50 Eggs Collected)

- Spawns chickens as allys
- Places normal eggs around him that act as barriers
- These eggs do not have special effects!

## Titan Basketman (Godly) (50 / 50 Eggs Collected)

- Punches toilets with his fist!
- Shoots explosive eggs from his shoulder that have 12 blast radius!
- Does 165K DPS!
