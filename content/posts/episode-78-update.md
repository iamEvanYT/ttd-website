Hello everyone, the Episode 78 Update is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# <u>Raids</u>

Jump into the brand-new Raid Arena and challenge yourself up to 3 runs every 12 hours. Even if you don’t make it to the end, you’ll still earn Raid Currency based on how long you survive—win or lose!

**Easy Raids**: Same challenge as standard Hard mode - perfect for learning the ropes.  
**Hard Raids**: Turn up the heat to Abysmal difficulty, with 2× higher chances for rare loot and extra Raid Currency.

## Rewards

Drops include Boosts, Coins, Gems, Reroll Shards, Raid Tickets and more soon!

## Refill Option

Need more attempts? You can replenish all 3 runs for just **99 Robux** before the timer resets - no pay-to-win, just a way to queue up more action if you’re on a roll.

## Raid Shop

The raid shop currently contains the Raid Crate with 4 new units!

---

# <u>Raid Crate</u>

This is a crate that can be purchased with 100 Raid Tickets each for some powerful units!

This is also the first crate to contain the **Celestial** Rarity which is better than Ultimates.

## Speaker Ship (Legendary - 50%)

Behold the Speaker Ship’s signature red beam - it scorches foes and simultaneously healing your units with a green beam.

## Headless TV Man (Mythic - 45%)

Although he has low range, he has high damage and slows toilets down to prevent them from reaching the end.

## Cross Speakerwoman (Godly - 4.9%)

Dual magenta blade-wielder incoming! She slices toilets with one cross-saber. She tears through enemy lines with piercing strikes and even shrugs off stuns better than most.

## Executioner Titan Speakerman (Celestial - 0.1%)

Your new big-damage centerpiece. He cleaves forward with a massive scythe arc that cleaves armor and crowds alike, then follows up from afar with splashy laser bursts when the enemy tries to back off. His built-in soundwave shockwave clears space in a wide cone, and a rotating astro-toilet ring on his back? Pure style. He’s the strongest unit we’ve ever unleashed—use him to smash through the toughest encounters (just keep an eye on his cooldowns and energy cost!).
