Hello toilet warriors, the St. Patricks Day Event is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# Lucky Toilets

These toilets can spawn on any mode except Endless, PvP or Speed Run Mode!

Their health works differently and they take 1 HP each time they are hit!

Every time a lucky toilet is killed you will be rewarded 10 Clovers.

There is a small chance that these toilets will spawn on any wave. You can upgrade the chance they spawn and the amount they spawn!

---

# Shamrock Shield Speakerman (Epic) (60%):

- Slashes toilets with its sword.
- Has a 10% chance that the toilet would get pushed back with its hits. (Only under 10k Strength)

# Pot O' Gold Cameraman (Legendary) (30%):

- Throws gold bars at toilets hurting them!
- Any toilet hit will turn to gold and when its killed you will get 2x the money!

# Titan Rainbow Clockman (Mythic) (8%):

- Blasts lasers from his palms!
- These laser balls will explode on impact! And hit a lot of the toilets in the radius (Doing Splash!)

# Titan Luckbringer Drillman (Godly) (2%):

- Drills at toilets! The drills go straight through them and can hit others its aimed at (Doing Piercing)
- At its highest level it gains the Assassin ability! This will instantly kill toilets under a certain health!

# Other Cool Units

## Lucky 3D Printman (Exclusive):

Picks up units then places them in the 3D Printer in his chest!

The units will be turned into allies.

There are strenth caps: 1k / 10k / 100k / 250k / 500k / 1m

Also punches toilets!
