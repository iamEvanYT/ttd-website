Hello everyone, the Italian Update (part 2) is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# <u>Italian Crate 2</u>

This is a new exclusive crate in the Exclusive Shop! You can purchase it for 300 gems each.

It is a continunity from the first Italian Crate, and contains 5 units!

###### Odds:

- Chimpanzini Bananini (50%)
- Bombombini Gusini (39.9%)
- Cappuccino Assassino (9%)
- Bombardiro Crocodilo (1%)
- Corrupt Bombardiro Crocodilo (0.1%)

## Chimpanzini Bananini (50%)

A green chimp in a banana peel! Bounces toward toilets with tiny hops and bites them on contact.

## Bombombini Gusini (39.9%)

A feisty little goose in a mini fighter plane! Hovers in place and fires small missiles to chip away at toilet swarms.

## Cappuccino Assassino (9%)

A caffeinated killer coffee cup! Dashes into toilets with sharp knives for fast, close-up attacks.

## Bombardiro Crocodilo (1%)

A bomb-dropping croc! Delivers splash damage from above and unlocks deadly missile attacks when leveled up.

## Corrupt Bombardiro Crocodilo (0.1%)

A corrupted, heavily armed version of Bombardiro! Sets toilets on fire with bombs and missiles, dealing damage over time and slowing them down.

# <u>Evolutions</u>

This is a new feature in Toilet Tower Defense!

To evolve units, you will need crafting currencies from Raids & Raid Shop. With these currencies, you can now evolve units to permanently increase their stats. Find the evolutions area next to traits rolling!

Units available for evolution:

- Speaker Ship
- Headless TV Man
- Cross Speakerwoman
- Hunter Astro Mutant
- Executioner Speakerman

# <u>Exclusive Items</u>

## Giraffa Meloniera (Unit)

This unit can be purchased in the Exclusive Shop for R$499.

A soaring giraffe-watermelon hybrid jet! Unleashes a barrage of explosive missiles and delivers powerful stuns to lock down big threats.

## Bombardino Crocodillo (Mount)

You can now purchase Bombardino Crocodillo as a mount for R$299, which you can ride on in the Lobby!
