The Japan Event is here in Toilet Tower Defense!

---

# Mount Fuji Map

In this map you can use Easy, Medium, Hard or Nightmare mode!

Each of these maps will give you flowers currency to get the new crate!

---

# Cherry Blossom Crate (100 Flowers)

## 🍣 Sushi Speakerman (60%) (Epic)

Throws Sushi at toilets.

Has 3 different types of sushi: Red, Orange, White!

The red sushi does fire damage! So the toilets will turn on fire and take damage over 10 seconds!

The white sushi slows the attackers

The orange sushi explodes on impact!

## 📷 Samurai TV Man (30%) (Legendary)

Does AOE damage hitting all toilets that are infront of him!

Slashes at toilets hitting multiple with one swipe (does piercing)

## 🥷 Ramen Chef TV Man (8%) (Mythic)

Cooks Ramen and throws it to other units in its area!

The bowls of Ramen boost and even heal your units!

## 🪙 Emperor Cameraman (2%) (Godly)

Spawns Samurai on the track!

These are some of the highest trained samurai.

When a toilet reaches the end of the track the emperor will dash over to it and stuns it for 10 seconds!

This will only work on stunable toilets.

---

# Other Cool Units

## Dual Blade Samurai Cameraman (Exclusive) (499 Robux)

Has a two katanas that he slashes toilets with!

There will be an initial slash that does the first part of the damage, then the second will do piercing!

So he can hit multiple toilets at once!

Does 135,000 DPS!
