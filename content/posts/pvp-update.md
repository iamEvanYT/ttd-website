# <u>New Features</u>

## PVP Mode

Normal pvp mode is in the lobby - across from Limited Time Modes!

Play 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, 4v4, or 5v5! Right now, each wave gets 15% harder than the last (endless is 2%), so it scales FAST!  
This is to make pvp matches only last usually 5-15 minutes, but for pros, matches could take up to maybe 20-30 or longer.  
This gives more coins than endless 1x speed - but a little less than 2x speed.

You only get a win if you beat wave 20 (and you win). This is so players can't challenge their alts, place nothing, and farm a win every 2 minutes!

![](/blog/images/2024/10/RobloxScreenShot20241026_140417202.webp)

## Shiny Units

All units now have a 1% chance to BE SHINY!  
All units in the summon, the shop, crates (including old crates) have this chance. (this will probably end up making old crates even rarer lol)

**Shiny Hunter** (R$499 - Gamepass)  
Shiny hunter increases your chance to 2% - still very rare!  
This adds another level to rarity & will set new goals for traders.

**Benefits**  
Shiny units have special effects and do 50% extra damage!  
Shiny ultimates have custom special effects that make them look even more unique and cooler!

## Pro Trading Plaza

There is now a Pro Trading Plaza designed for rich players!

To enter it, you must have over 1 million gems / own an Ultimate.  
Good Luck!

# <u>Halloween Part 2</u>

The halloween shop has a new crate - the Haunted Crate!  
It's a little more expensive than the first crate, but it has higher godly chances!

###### Odds:

- 95% - Skeleton Cammeraman
- 4% - Grim Reaperman
- 1% - Titan Doomspeaker

![](/blog/images/2024/10/image-5.webp)

## Skeleton Cameraman (Legendary - 95%)

- Attacks with a haunted sword!
- 10% chance every attack to "scare" a toilet! It runs away!

## Grim Reaperman (Mythic - 4%)

- Swings scythe at toilets
- 360 degree AoE fire circle

## Titan Doomspeaker (Godly - 1%)

- Attacks toilets with scythe
- 10% chance to INSTANTLY kill a toilet under 10% health (every second). This attack can do over a million damage under the right circumstances.

# <u>Others</u>

## Titan Alien Man (Exclusive)

This unit can be found in the Exclusive Shop for 699 Robux.

- Spawns UFOs to abduct toilets! Doesn't work on very large or very strong toilets. They are too strong to abduct.
- If a toilet is abducted, it can't be abducted again for 10 more seconds - if it survives. When it gets pulled up it crashes into the UFO!
- He also has a laser gun!
- Launches a parasite that slows toilets nearby.

## Placement Rewrite

The placement system has been recoded! It now has more accurate hitboxes which will make it easier to place units.
